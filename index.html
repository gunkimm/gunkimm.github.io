Gun Kim (Dong Gun Kim) is a research-based practitioner working with 3D media. He studied architecture (BSA) at the State University of New York at Buffalo and received an MFA in Visual Communication Design from the Department of Design, College of Fine Arts, Seoul National University. His method is an interdisciplinary approach that crosses critical theory with visual practice. He runs the independent publishing collective Publishing Neighbors and works as a ZER01NE creator with Hyundai Motor Company.

The theoretical ground of his work is set out in his MFA thesis, Morphology of Disarmament: When Cuteness Becomes Power, which traces how cuteness, as an aesthetic form, structures power through an appearance of nonviolence and harmlessness.
